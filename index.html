06 / BEHIND THE REDLINE

MEET THE RIDER.
RISHAV AKA JATAYIU.

Not just about the destination. Always about the journey.

I'm Rishav, known on the road as Jatayiu — a motorcycle enthusiast, solo rider and storyteller with an enduring love for machines and the open road.

My journey has taken me across the winding roads of Uttarakhand, through Himalayan landscapes, unfamiliar towns and some seriously long days in the saddle. From covering 1,400 kilometres in a solo 20-hour ride to exploring the mountains and camping along the way, every ride has left something behind.

My Royal Enfield Classic 350 has been more than just a motorcycle. It's been a companion through countless kilometres, changing landscapes and lessons that no specification sheet can explain.

Away from the road, I'm fascinated by motorcycle engineering, design, mechanical character and the history behind iconic machines.

That's what REDLINE PAGES is about — documenting motorcycles, understanding engineering, exploring riding culture and telling stories that deserve to be told.

RIDE FAR. STAY CURIOUS. — JATAYIU
