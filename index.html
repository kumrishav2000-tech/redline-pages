06 / BEHIND THE REDLINE

MEET THE RIDER.
RISHAV AKA JATAYIU.

Not just about the destination. Always about the journey.

I'm Rishav, known on the road as Jatayiu — a motorcycle enthusiast, solo long-distance rider, and storyteller with an enduring respect for classic mechanics and open horizons.

My rides have spanned sweeping alpine twisties across Uttarakhand, remote passes, and demanding endurance trips including a solo 1,400-kilometre, 20-hour push.

My Royal Enfield Classic 350 has served as an unflinching companion through every storm, altitude change, and high-mileage day. REDLINE PAGES is where engineering insight and road stories unite.

RIDE FAR. STAY CURIOUS. — JATAYIU
