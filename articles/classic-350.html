1948 — 2026 / A LEGACY IN MOTION
THE MACHINE THAT BECAME A MOVEMENT

Not built to follow.
Built to endure.

The Royal Enfield Classic 350 occupies a special place in India's motorcycling landscape. Its teardrop tank, upright stance, metal detailing and unmistakable silhouette draw from a post-war design language, while its engineering has evolved across generations.

This is its story — from the Model G2 inspiration to the modern J-platform and the latest 2026 updates.

WHAT MAKES IT SPECIAL?

01 / DESIGN

The silhouette

The teardrop fuel tank, round headlamp, flowing fenders, upright riding position and vintage-inspired detailing give the Classic its instantly recognisable identity.

02 / ENGINEERING

Evolution underneath

From the earlier UCE engine to the modern J-series platform, the motorcycle has evolved while preserving its approachable character.

03 / CULTURE

More than transportation

The Classic became a familiar sight across Indian cities, highways and touring communities. Its identity extends beyond its specification sheet.

04 / PERSONALITY

A canvas for its rider

Paint schemes, accessories, touring equipment and customisation have helped riders make the Classic their own.
